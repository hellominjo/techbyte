## How to use it

1. **Search or pick a category.**

    Search in Korean or English, including full names and descriptions.
2. **Get the gist.**

    The summary and analogy alone are enough to follow a conversation.

3. **Follow the links.**

    Labels guide you naturally to the next idea.

4. **Learn one term a day.**

    Start with "Today's IT term" and "Popular IT terms" on the home page.
